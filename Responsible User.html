What Makes a Responsible User?

A responsible user understands their digital footprint and makes thoughtful choices when interacting online. They protect their privacy, respect others, and contribute positively to online communities.

This section highlights key practices that define responsible online behavior and help create a safer digital environment for everyone.

Key Responsible Practices

Think Before You Share

Consider the impact of your posts, comments, and shares. Ask yourself: Is this true? Is it kind? Is it necessary?

Protect Your Privacy

Use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and be cautious about sharing personal details online.

Verify Before Believing

Check sources, look for evidence, and be skeptical of sensational claims or too-good-to-be-true offers.
